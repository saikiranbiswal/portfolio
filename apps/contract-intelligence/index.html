Contract Intelligence Platform

01 — Problem

The answers are in the contracts. Nobody can find them.

Interviews across legal, finance & procurement.

Enterprises sign thousands of contracts and then lose them — in inboxes, shared drives, and PDFs no one re-reads. Renewal dates slip, auto-renew clauses bite, and obligations go untracked until they become disputes. Legal review is a bottleneck, and "where does it say that?" takes days to answer.

01Buried obligationsKey dates and duties trapped in unstructured documents.
02Review bottleneckEvery question routes through over-stretched legal teams.
03Risk found too lateProblems surface at renewal or in dispute, not before.
02 — Strategy

Turn documents into a queryable knowledge base.

Vision & approach.

Vision — Every contract becomes structured, searchable, and monitored — so any team can ask a question in plain language and get a cited answer.

Approach — Lead with extraction + obligation tracking (the painkiller), then layer conversational search once trust in the data is established.
