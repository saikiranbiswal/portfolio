Case study · 2022–2023

Loan Origination System

A modern, configurable LOS that compresses application-to-disbursal from days to minutes — without losing the controls a lender's risk team depends on.

Role
Senior Product Manager

01 — Problem

A funnel that leaked at every step.

Discovery with ops, risk & 30 applicants.

The legacy LOS was a monolith: every new product or policy change meant a release, KYC was re-keyed across three systems, and 40% of applicants dropped before disbursal. Risk couldn't change a rule without engineering, and partners couldn't integrate without months of bespoke work.

01Rigid product setupLaunching a new loan product took a full release cycle.
02Fragmented KYCApplicants re-entered the same data three times.
03Opaque decisioningRules lived in code; risk teams flew blind.
02 — Strategy

Configuration over code.

Vision & the platform bet.

Vision — An origination platform where business teams configure products, journeys, and rules — and engineering builds capabilities, not one-off flows.

The bet — A workflow engine + rules engine at the core, with every integration behind a stable API contract. Ship one product on it, then migrate the rest.

03 — Domain Model

One lifecycle, cleanly modeled.

Entity model.

04 — Architecture

Engines at the core, APIs at the edge.

Component & integration view.

A workflow engine drives the journey; a rules engine owns eligibility and pricing; everything external — bureau, KYC, core banking, payments — sits behind versioned contracts.
