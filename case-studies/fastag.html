FASTag Redesign, National Toll & Mobility Trust Layer

Redesign FASTag as a National Toll and Mobility Account: one trusted place to confirm toll readiness, understand deductions, resolve disputes, manage annual passes, and protect fleet uptime.

01

Problem

The sticker works. The system around it does not.

Users cannot reliably tell whether a vehicle is toll-ready, why a tag is blocked, whether an annual pass applied, or where a disputed deduction sits. The experience is split across issuer banks, plazas, NHAI/IHMCL services, and complaint channels — so every exception becomes a manual chase.

Why it matters

Failed and disputed crossings erode trust in a national payment rail, generate complaint volume, and leak revenue — while the underlying tag technology is fine.

03

Domain model

Model the toll account, not the sticker.

Get these objects and their lifecycle right and the screens, disputes, and AI fall into place. The redesign is built on six core objects with explicit states.

OBJECT

Vehicle & Tag

States: active, low-balance, blacklisted, KYC-pending — each with a reason code and a fix-now action.

OBJECT

Toll Account

Balance, KYC, and linked vehicles — the single source of toll-readiness truth.

OBJECT

Toll Event

Plaza, class, amount, payment source, pass applied, and supporting evidence — the explainable ledger entry.

OBJECT

Pass

Eligibility, trips remaining, validity, and route impact — resolved before travel.

OBJECT

Dispute

Reason, auto-assembled evidence pack, SLA, owner, and outcome — governed end to end.

OBJECT

Exception

Failed read, duplicate, class mismatch, or ANPR conflict — queued for operator approval.

04

AI design

TollTrust Copilot, inside a safe boundary.

AI is a product decision here, not a model choice. The copilot accelerates diagnosis and drafting; consequential, irreversible decisions stay human- or rule-governed.

The copilot does

  • Diagnose a blocked or inactive tag into a specific root cause and severity.
  • Explain a deduction in plain language from the event ledger.
  • Draft a dispute with the evidence pack already assembled.
  • Surface pass eligibility and the next-best action before travel.

Humans & rules govern

  • Penalties and blacklisting decisions.
  • Refund approval and rejection.
  • Government toll pricing — never changed by the product.
  • Operator exception sign-off on contested reads.

Operator exceptions

Prioritize failed reads, duplicates, class mismatches, and ANPR conflicts with human approval.

Out of scope, on purpose

No new wallet, no nationwide GNSS replacement, no changes to government toll pricing, and no fully autonomous penalty, blacklist, or refund decisions.

  1. Check readinessVehicle, issuer, KYC, balance, tag, and pass status resolve into one health state.
  2. Cross with confidenceThe event ledger explains what the plaza read and what was charged or applied.
  3. Challenge an exceptionA dispute starts with a reason and an auto-assembled evidence pack.
  4. Track the outcomeStatus, owner, SLA, refund, and next action stay visible without channel hopping.
