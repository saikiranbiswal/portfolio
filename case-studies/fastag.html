FASTag Redesign, National Toll & Mobility Trust Layer

Redesign FASTag as a National Toll and Mobility Account: one trusted place to confirm toll readiness, understand deductions, resolve disputes, manage annual passes, and protect fleet uptime.

Role
Product Manager · Strategy & PRD
Stage
Strategy + PRD
Primary users
Drivers, commuters, fleets, toll operators
North Star
Successful toll crossings without user intervention
01

The frame

The sticker works. The system around it does not.

Users cannot reliably tell whether a vehicle is toll-ready, why a tag is blocked, whether an annual pass applied, or where a disputed deduction sits. The experience is split across issuer banks, plazas, NHAI/IHMCL services, and complaint channels.

Not solving

No new wallet, no nationwide GNSS replacement, no changes to government toll pricing, and no fully autonomous penalty, blacklist, or refund decisions.

02

The wedge

Win on trust, diagnostics, and interoperability.

01

FASTag Health

Turn “inactive” or “blacklisted” into a specific root cause, severity, and fix-now action.

02

Explainable ledger

Show plaza, class, amount, payment source, pass application, and supporting evidence for every event.

03

Dispute workflow

Package the evidence automatically, expose SLA status, and keep refunds and rejections governed.

04

Pass intelligence

Make eligibility, trips left, validity, route impact, and pass application obvious before travel.

05

Fleet control

Aggregate vehicle health, low balance, toll spend, and disputes for bulk action.

06

Operator exceptions

Prioritize failed reads, duplicates, class mismatches, and ANPR conflicts with human approval.

03

MVP journey

A pre-trip check that continues through resolution.

  1. Check readinessVehicle, issuer, KYC, balance, tag, and pass status resolve into one health state.
  2. Cross with confidenceThe event ledger explains what the plaza read and what was charged or applied.
  3. Challenge an exceptionA dispute starts with a reason and an auto-assembled evidence pack.
  4. Track the outcomeStatus, owner, SLA, refund, and next action stay visible without channel hopping.
04

Success

Measure predictable crossings, not app engagement.

The MVP succeeds when the vehicle crosses, the correct amount or entitlement is applied, and the user does not need manual intervention.

< 60 secTarget time to raise a complete dispute
One statusVehicle health with reason and recovery action
One ledgerEvery toll event, pass use, and deduction explained

The deliverable is a strategy and PRD, not a claim of a shipped national platform.
