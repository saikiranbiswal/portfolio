About

From problem to platform, end to end.

I started in business analysis — close enough to operations to see exactly where they break — and grew into product strategy and 0→1 platform work.

A real product needs a clear problem, an honest business case, a clean domain model, an architecture engineering can build, an experience people adopt, and a commercial model that holds up. I'm comfortable owning all of it. Lately my focus is AI-native products that ship and move a number — not artifacts that sit in a deck.

Model the domain first

Get the objects and lifecycle right and the screens, data, and AI fall into place.

Earn the next feature

Lead with the painkiller, prove value, then expand. Trust compounds; scope creep doesn't.

AI is a product decision

Use case, trust, evaluation, guardrails, and cost beat whichever model is trending.

Tie it to a number

A north-star metric and an honest unit-economics model keep a roadmap defensible.
